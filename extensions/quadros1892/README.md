## Conventions

* Spelling follows Portuguese orthography of the time; Quadros gives no key. On p. 255 he remarks that "os dos Xavantes têm quasi todas o accento agudo na penultima" (almost all Xavante words carry the acute accent on the penultimate).
* `Value` = the printed entry, including its final full stop (every Xavante entry ends in "."); `Form` drops that stop. `Segments` empty.
* Alignment: rows were matched to Portuguese glosses through the Coroado column and the row leaders. On p. 259 the Caiuá column is offset by one row, but the Coroado and Xavante columns align with the Portuguese one (confirmed by Kaingang Coroado forms and by Borba's *Apalaiao* 'cobra').
* Parameter `Name` = Portuguese gloss as printed | English. "Velho", "Indio do mato", "Campo", "Queixada", "Caitetú" and "Perdiz" (a Brazilian tinamou, not a partridge) are unmapped.

## Counts and exclusions

43 glosses with a Xavante form → 43 rows; 37 mapped. Excluded: 55 Portuguese glosses (p. 256: 12, p. 257: 16, p. 258: 7, p. 259: 20) whose Xavante cell is a dash "—" or blank (e.g. Deos, Chuva, Rio, Olho, Mão, Pé, all of p. 259 after Cobra); no proper names, no phrases.

## Identity and caveats

Quadros's "Xavante" = **Oti** (otii1244), the São Paulo Chavantes — not Akwẽ Xavante. Several rows disagree with Borba 1908 (e.g. Quadros *Acóli* 'orelha' vs Borba *Acli* 'olho'); they are transcribed as printed and noted, not corrected. Quadros's "Coroado" is Kaingang and not part of this extension.
