# Seler 1902: the language of the Indians of Esmeraldas (Atacame / Esmeraldeño)

**Source.** Eduard Seler, "Nachtrag B. Die Sprache der Indianer von Esmeraldas", in
*Gesammelte Abhandlungen zur amerikanischen Sprach- und Alterthumskunde*, vol. I
(Berlin: Asher, 1902), pp. 49–64. The vocabulary (pp. 54–60) was collected in 1877 by
J. M. Pallares at Esmeraldas (Ecuador) for Theodor Wolf, from the last elderly speakers.
Seler arranges it by topic, adds Spanish glosses as written by Pallares, and adds his own
corrections and readings in square brackets and German notes.

**Scans.** archive.org `gesammelteabhand00sele`
(https://archive.org/download/gesammelteabhand00sele/gesammelteabhand00sele.pdf, sha256
`550e53519959db4750b7612d55e4695faa1409c07b22ccffc543acc87ca636ff`), PDF pages 85–93.
A second copy (archive.org `gesammelteabhan01selegoog`, sha256
`afb3c48aa7f42b69130b147ac7c0503ba44bd7024896b7c900b4bb74023e2723`, PDF pages 86–93)
was used for the second reading and for the start of the left column on p. 59, which is
lost in the gutter of the first scan. Both are in `sources/atac1235/`.

**Pages transcribed.** pp. 54–60 (the vocabulary). From pp. 61–62 (Seler's grammatical
regrouping) only items not in the vocabulary were added: *aná* 'este', *ané* 'como',
*amata, ata* 'el, ella', *ba-l-e* 'no, no hay', *bashini* 'uno'.

**Variety.** One: `seler1902_esmeraldas`, Glottocode `atac1235` (Atacame).

**Transcription.** Transcribed by an AI agent from page images on 2026-11-04; verified by
a second independent reading (see `VERIFY.md`). Forms are as printed in Seler's italics,
with accents, diaeresis, hyphens (Seler's morpheme division) and Seler's nasal hook
(rendered as U+0361 COMBINING DOUBLE INVERTED BREVE across two letters, e.g. `tia͡una`,
`paa͡nta`; Seler p. 53: "Wo der Aufzeichner eine nasale Aussprache angemerkt hat, habe
ich ein Häkchen gesetzt", in the note introducing the vocabulary). Single-vowel bars are kept as macron (`sháīmajilo`,
`shul-paāta`), flagged unclear. `Value` keeps the entry as printed (including Seler's
bracketed insertions, e.g. `y[w]-gualpho-só`); `Form` drops bracketed material. Where an
entry lists alternatives (`a, b`, `a ó b`, Seler's `a oder b`) there is one row per
alternative. Pallares's and Seler's notes go in `Comment` (prefixed `Pallares:` /
`Seler:`). `Segments` is empty. The italic r/v and t/l are often hard to tell apart in
the scans; Jijón y Caamaño's respellings (via the Wiktionary appendix) were used only to
decide between visually possible letters, never as the form; such cases carry
`Doubt = true`.

**Concepts.** `Name` = Pallares's Spanish gloss as printed (with Seler's brackets, e.g.
`[mi] hijo`), plus an English translation. 401 glosses; 243 mapped to Concepticon.
Possessed body-part and kin nouns (`[mi] nariz`) are mapped to the plain concept;
inflected verbs, phrases with person marking, and generic/specific mismatches are not.

**Loans.** `Loan = true` only where Seler marks the word as Spanish or Quechua:
*güalpa* (Quichuá), *cansalene* (span.), *aburralene* (spanisch), *inganijonegue*
(span. engañando), *fechini-ssa* (spanisch fecho). *tia-sa* "(span.?)" is noted, not
flagged.

**Exclusions (205 printed lines).** 122 phrases and sentences; 78 repeated entries (Seler
lists many items under several headings; the first occurrence is kept, variant spellings
are noted); 3 Seler section headings (*ilom* Mann, *tin-* Frau, *iu-* Kind); 1 proper
name (*Chinto*, old name of the Esmeraldas river); 1 form without gloss (*imulichelene*).

**Not done.** The 37 forms in Wolf 1892, p. 528 (downloaded, `sources/atac1235/wolf1892_ia`)
were not collated.

**Licence.** Published 1902 (US public domain; Seler died 1922) →

**Identity issues.** Semi-speaker data recorded by a non-linguist; many glosses are
inflected forms. Several entries are near-duplicates with variant spellings across pages
(e.g. *viacúle* ~ *viaccúle* 'fiebre'); only the first is kept.
