# nimuendaju1925: Matanawí vocabulary (Nimuendajú 1925)

**Source.** Nimuendajú, Curt. 1925. "As tribus do Alto Madeira." *Journal de la
Société des Américanistes* 17: 137–172. Section V, "Vocabulario Matanawí",
printed pp. 166–171. The Mura-Pirahã list ends on p. 166 above the heading; §VI
"Vocabulario Ntogapid" (a Tupian list) starts on p. 172. Neither is included.

**Scan.** archive.org item `societe-des-americanistes-journal_1925_17` (all of
vol. 17):
<https://archive.org/download/societe-des-americanistes-journal_1925_17/societe-des-americanistes-journal_1925_17.pdf>,
sha256 `dc8929c57ca381af8879db6b16690cdf54f2379a1806e3e0e21214e551b9d8f5`.
Printed p. 166 = PDF p. 192 (PDF page = printed + 26). The page images used are in
`pages/` (150 dpi, named by printed page).

**Variety.** One variety, `matanawi` → Matanawí, `mata1275`. Nimuendajú's heading
(p. 166): "Organisado com o indio *Matanawį* João Comprido, morador em Surupy, no
Baixo Marmellos, em Janeiro e Novembro de 1922." ('Compiled with the Matanawí
Indian João Comprido, living at Surupy on the lower Marmellos, in January and
November 1922.') A single speaker and a single collector; no other primary
source for the language is known.

**Who and how.** Transcribed by an AI agent from page images on 2026-11-04;
verified by a second independent reading. The archive.org text layer is not
used: its OCR loses the special letters. Pages were rendered at 430–1100 dpi
(the scan's native resolution is about 365 dpi).

## Nimuendajú's notation key

The only notation key printed with the list is the page footnote, repeated on
each page:

> 1. ǫ, tontragender Vokal.
> 2. ų, tontragender Vokal.

(German: 'ǫ / ų, tone-bearing [i.e. stressed] vowel'.) The footnote number is
attached in the list to forms containing such a vowel, e.g. *ñatu zį oǫ¹*,
*iwahų²*. The article gives no other key. The other letters are Nimuendajú's
usual Americanist letters (č, š, ñ, y, w, z, accents, tildes); their values are
not stated in this article and are not interpreted here.

## Conventions

* `Value` is the full printed entry, footnote numbers (¹ ²) and printed
  punctuation included. Where an entry runs over several printed lines without
  a separator, the lines are joined with ` / ` (3 entries: Labio, meu irmão
  maior, dentro da casa).
* `Form` is one alternative, without footnote numbers or trailing commas.
  Alternatives separated by a comma or printed on separate lines are separate
  rows with the same `Value`.
* The mark printed under vowels (ǫ, į, ų, ę, ą, the "tontragender Vokal" of the
  footnote) is a small hook. It is transcribed with the combining ogonek
  (U+0328). It is **not** nasality here; it is Nimuendajú's stress mark.
* Overbars on vowels are transcribed as tildes (U+0303). At the scan's
  resolution a tilde and a macron are not always distinguishable; where the
  shape was clear it was wavy (e.g. *roo nãro*, *kasã*), the article defines no
  length mark for this list, and Nimuendajú elsewhere marks nasality with a
  tilde. Several vowels carry a stacked bar and acute; these are transcribed
  `ã́` (tilde + acute). Breve (*mõzĕaší*), circumflex (*ñô*, *įrįpįrân*,
  *ñamârį*) and grave (*tawà*, *tomà zį*, *natamà*) are transcribed as printed.
* The italic *h* and *b* of this typeface are close; *h* was read except in the
  `-bm`/`-bn` clusters (*turusikábm*, *mįríbm*), after comparison at high
  magnification (e.g. *hotó*, *ųrurę́hm*).
* An italic i-dot and an acute were distinguished by stroke length; residual
  uncertainty is noted below.
* `Segments` is empty for every row. This is a pre-IPA Americanist notation, not
  IPA; any profile is a later, reviewed step.
* `Loan` = `true` only where the form is identical to the regional Portuguese
  gloss: *tušáu* 'Tuxáua' (chief) and *tipití* 'Tipity'. No other loans are marked.
* Kin terms keep the speaker-sex marks (♂, ♀) of the gloss. Possessed forms
  ("minha mulher", "meu arco") are kept as their own glosses and are unmapped.
* Concepticon mapping is conservative: possessed and sex-of-speaker kin terms,
  specific fauna/flora without an exact concept, Amazonian "inverno"/"verão"
  (rainy/dry season), "terra", "carne", "ouvido", "alto/baixo", "velho" and
  forms that look inflected ("coçar" *wįhana ru*) are unmapped.

## Identity notes

* Matanawí (`mata1275`) is unambiguous. The glosses "Arára (Aripuanã)" and
  "Arára (R. Preto)" are ethnonyms and are excluded; they are not evidence for
  Mato Grosso Arára (`mato1253`).
* The Mura-Pirahã list on the same page and the Ntogapid list on p. 172 are other
  languages and are not included.

**Convention (blind check, 2026-11-04):** the bar over vowels is written as a tilde; the scan does not distinguish a tilde from a macron, so this is a transcription convention, not a reading.
