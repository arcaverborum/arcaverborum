## Notation and conventions

Nimuendajú's Americanist typescript notation: š, ž (háček), ń (n with acute, before g), tilde for nasality, acute for stress, circumflex, breve (handwritten or overstruck), and a slash "/" inside Fulniô forms whose value he does not explain here (kept as printed). Annotations "= (bom", "= (mel", "= (carneiro" belong to the Šukurú cells (Nimuendajú's gloss refinements); they are kept in `Value` and in `Comment`, and removed from `Form`. `Segments` empty.

Diacritics on this low-resolution facsimile are frequently uncertain, especially the distinction between breve and tilde over e/o; every such row carries `unclear:` in `Comment`. Where the Šukurú form is also printed by Loukotka 1949, his reading is cited in the comment for comparison but **not** used to change the transcription.
