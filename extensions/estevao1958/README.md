# estevao1958 — Tuxá ("Tushá"), Estêvão's words printed by Pompeu Sobrinho 1958

**Source.** Words collected by **Carlos Estêvão [de Oliveira]** from "Antônio Likaro e Cordorina, caboclos da Tribo Tushá, Rodelas" (Bahia). Estêvão entrusted five small vocabularies to Th. Pompeu Sobrinho in 1940; Pompeu printed them in: Pompeu Sobrinho, Thomaz. 1958. "Línguas Tapuias desconhecidas do Nordeste: alguns vocabulários inéditos." *Boletim de Antropologia* (Fortaleza) 2(1): 3–19. Attribution p. 9 ("Eis em primeiro lugar os vocabulários do Dr. Carlos Estêvam"); "Vocabulário nº 3" pp. 12–13.

**Preparation.** Transcribed by an AI agent from page images on 2026-11-04; verified by a second independent reading (see `VERIFY.md`). **Only Estêvão's forms and Portuguese glosses are taken**; Pompeu's footnotes and commentary are not reproduced (footnote (10) on *Junkurun*, "Virá daí o nome Sukuru?", is editorial; its marker "(10)" is kept in `Value` only because it is printed in the entry).

## Identity and caveats

Tushá = Tuxá (Rodela), tuxa1239. Pompeu warns (p. 9) that in these lists, gathered among mixed remnant groups, words of unknown languages sit beside Tupi and Fulniô words; *Tôrú* 'cachimbo' is Fulniô-like. Do not confuse with the modern Tuxá revival of Dzubukuá (Kariri). Not done: Estêvão's other lists in the same article (nº 1 Brejo dos Padres, 59 words; nº 2 Serra Negra) — outside this task's scope; nº 1 overlaps Nimuendajú's Macarú column.
