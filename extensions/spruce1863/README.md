# Spruce 1863: Sechura wordlist (edition: Urban 2015)

**Source.** Richard Spruce, *Words in the language of Morrope, Colan, Eten, Sechura and
Catacaos*, manuscript, 1863 (Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, RSP/3/1), as edited by Matthias
Urban, "El vocabulario sechurano de Richard Spruce", *Lexis* 39(2): 395–413 (2015),
doi:10.18800/lexis.201502.005. Pages: Appendix A (pp. 409–411, Urban's transcription) and
Appendix B (pp. 412–413, facsimile of the two manuscript pages). PDF:
https://revistas.pucp.edu.pe/index.php/lexis/article/download/14606/15204, sha256

`sources/sech1236/urban2015-lexis-spruce/`).

**Variety.** One: `spruce1863_sechura`, Glottocode `sech1236` (Sechuran, language level).
The manuscript title names Mórrope, Colán, Eten, Sechura and Catacaos; attributing the
list to Sechura is Urban's working hypothesis, and the locality (Sechura or Olmos) is not
certain, so the dialect code `secc1234` is not used. No Colán list is present in the
manuscript (only the title mentions Colán).

**What was taken.** Spruce's original forms and English glosses only. `Form` follows
the manuscript as seen in the facsimile, with Spruce's capital initials (underlines not
reproduced); letters were checked against Urban's 2015 transcription, which lower-cases
the initials. A space written inside a form (`Coyu roro`) is kept. Urban's Spanish translations, notes and comparisons with Buchwald, Jijón and
Rivet are not copied; where Urban gives an alternative reading it is noted in `Comment`
with `Doubt = true`. Spruce's Mochica comparison forms written to the right of some
entries (e.g. beside Man, Woman, Head, Eye, Mouth) are not transcribed. `Segments` empty.

Transcribed by an AI agent from page images on 2026-11-04; verified by a second
independent reading (see `VERIFY.md`).

**Exclusions (9 of 40 glosses).** 7 phrases, imperatives or greetings (Come here, Be
quiet, Come along, Good day, How are you, Where is your husband, Here it is); 1 gloss
without a form (Plantain). Kept: 31 rows, 31 glosses, 29 mapped to Concepticon
(Hearing and Light unmapped).

**Licence.** The 1863 manuscript is public domain (Spruce died 1893); the edition
(Urban 2015) is CC BY 4.0. The extension is released as CC BY 4.0
(`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`), citing both.

**Identity issues.** Earlier editions (Buchwald 1918, Lehmann 1920, Jijón y Caamaño 1945,
Rivet 1949) contain serious misreadings (hawk → "heart", hearing → "herring", face →
"house"); they were not used. `roncho` "turkey buzzard" may be a Spanish loan (not
flagged). The Plan's Sechura column is a separate extension (`martinezcompanon1790`); the
two lists overlap in few concepts and are written by different hands a century apart.
