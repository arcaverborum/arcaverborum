# nimuendaju1949 — Šukurú (Xukurú), Nimuendajú's 1934 words printed by Loukotka 1949

**Source.** Nimuendajú, Curt. [1934]. *Vokabular der Šukurú-Sprache vom Cimbres, Pernambuco* (manuscript, now lost). In 1934 Nimuendajú found four old villagers at Cimbres who still knew some words; they dictated 137 words. Extracts were sent by Nimuendajú himself to Č. Loukotka in 1940 and printed in: Loukotka, Čestmír. 1949. "Sur quelques langues inconnues de l'Amérique du Sud." *Lingua Posnaniensis* 1: 53–82, §15 "Šukurú", pp. 79–80.

## Related material not made into extensions

* Pompeu Sobrinho 1958 (*Boletim de Antropologia* 2(1), pp. 17–18) reprints "o vocabulário Xucurú colegido pelo Sr. Kurt Nimuendajú" (15 words) from the *Revista do Instituto Arqueológico, Histórico e Geográfico Pernambucano* nos. 155/158. It is the same 1934 dataset in a Portuguese-style respelling (e.g. *Xenúpre*, *Intôa*, *Kébra*), with three glosses not in Loukotka (*Bôca* Mãz, *Nariz* Korõzá, *Ôlho* Pigó). Because it is a respelled second-hand reprint whose original printing was not obtained, it is noted here rather than added as an extension.
* The 1935 typescript (`nimuendaju1935`) has 11 Šukurú forms from the same fieldwork.

## Identity

Šukurú = Xukurú of Ororubá (Cimbres / Pesqueira, Pernambuco), xuku1239. Hohenthal (1954) suggested that some later-recorded forms may be Paratió; the language-level code is used rather than a dialect code.
