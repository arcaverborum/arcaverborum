# Martínez Compañón's "Plan" (c. 1782–1790): Yunga, Sechura, Colán, Catacaos and Culli

**Source.** Baltasar Jaime Martínez Compañón, *Plan que contiene 43. vozes Castellanas
traducidas ãlas õcho lenguas que hablan los Yndios de la Costa, Sierras, y Montañas del
Obp̃do. de Trugillo del Perù*, manuscript plate in *Trujillo del Perú*, vol. II, estampa IV
(Real Biblioteca, Madrid, II/344). One plate, 43 Spanish glosses (Dios … Pez, ò Pescado),
nine columns: Castellana, Quichua, Yunga, Sechura, Colán, Catacaos, Culli, Hivito, Cholón.

This extension transcribes five columns, one variety each:

| ID | Column heading on the plate | Glottocode |
|---|---|---|
| `martinezcompanon1790_yunga` | Lengua Yunga de las Provincias de Trugillo, y Saña | moch1259 (Mochica) |
| `martinezcompanon1790_sechura` | Lengua de Sechura en la Provincia de Piura | secc1234 (Sec, dialect of Sechuran sech1236) |
| `martinezcompanon1790_colan` | Lengua de Colan en la Provincia de Piura | cola1238 (Colán, dialect of Tallán tall1235) |
| `martinezcompanon1790_catacaos` | Lengua de Catacaos en la Provincia de Piura | cata1293 (Catacaos, dialect of Tallán) |
| `martinezcompanon1790_culli` | Lengua Culli de la Provincia de Guamachuco | cull1235 (Culli) |

The Yunga (Mochica) column is a historical witness only; it is not meant to replace the
current Mochica sources. Quichua, Hivito and Cholón are not transcribed here.

**Transcription.** Transcribed by an AI agent from page images on 2026-11-04; verified by
a second independent reading (see `VERIFY.md`). Forms are as written on the plate,
including capital initials, accents (acute, grave), tildes (over vowels, n, m, s, g),
carons (č, ǎ, ǧ). Where the plate prints a star-shaped mark directly above a letter
(Yunga column only, e.g. `Og⃰`, `Gã⃰`, `Hã⃰n`), it is written with U+20F0 COMBINING ASTERISK
ABOVE after that letter; where its position is unclear it is described in `Comment` and
not reproduced (Culli `Vrù sagars`, Yunga `Chetes`, `Mechen`). The values of these
diacritics are unknown (Rivet 1949; Urban 2019: 79–80); nothing is interpreted. Readings
in doubt carry `Doubt = true` and an `unclear:` comment. Cells with alternatives (`Col, ò
Cog⃰`; `Lá, o Gã⃰`; `Og⃰, u, ol`) keep the full cell in `Value` and give one row per
alternative. `Segments` is empty for every row.

**Concepts.** 43 glosses, printed Spanish + English. 42 mapped to Concepticon; `Tierra`
(earth/land/soil) is left unmapped as ambiguous. `Carne` → FLESH OR MEAT; `Yerva` → GRASS.

**Loans.** `Loan = true` only for forms that are evidently Spanish words (Alma, Cuerpo,
Cuerpu, Carne, Animal, Arbol, Flor, Fruto, Cielo, Estrellas, Olas, Gozo, Rama, Dioós, Tios̃,
Thios, Cosecham, Almacchi, Cuerpocchi, Animblà, Florac). Probable loans not flagged but
noted in `Comment`: Yunga Yos (Dios), Catacaos Pateri, Culli Aycha, Mamǎ, Challuǎ
(Quechua).

**Exclusions.** None: every cell of the five columns is transcribed (43 × 5 cells,
219 rows). No phrases, names or unreadable cells.

**Identity issues.** Colán and Catacaos are two lists of the same glosses from
neighbouring villages and must not count as independent confirmation. Sechura proper is
coded `secc1234` (Glottolog's Sec dialect); use `sech1236` if language-level codes are
required. The Madrid and Bogotá copies of the Plan differ (Urban 2019 tables); this is the
Madrid copy only.
